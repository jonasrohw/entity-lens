MOR GEVA BASELINE
Mor Geva also has an important baseline that we probably
should replicate. She builds an index of Wikipedia, uses a
subject as a query on this index, retrieves k documents and
then builds a ranked vocabulary list for these k
documents. She then compares this to logit lens predictions
for a subject.  Sebastian rebuilt her framework and used it
in this EMNLP paper:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18612
Unfortunately, Sebastian is gone over the weekend, but I
think we should talk with him on Monday and try and reuse
his code to put this baseline in the paper.
